Connect first, move later

Passwords
AD password hashes don’t export in a form Auth0 can use. Staff usually keep signing in against AD through Auth0’s AD/LDAP connector, so nobody resets a password.
Second factors
Phone numbers and email factors carry over. Passkeys and push apps are tied to the old domain and always need re-enrolling.
User IDs
You can keep each user’s old ID on import. Auth0 adds a prefix (auth0|…), so apps that compare the raw sub claim need a small change.
Watch for
Auth0 accepts ADFS as a WS-Fed connection directly, so your apps can move before your users do.
