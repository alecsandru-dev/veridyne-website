Who we are

This website is run by VERIDYNE SOLUTIONS S.R.L. (CUI 51635423, Reg. Com. J2025027184005), Str. Guliver nr. 13, Bucharest, Romania. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: solutions@veridyne.eu.

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, company (if you give it), the topic you pick and your message. We use them only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to prepare and manage our work together.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records technical data such as IP address, browser and time of request, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Veridyne Team for Chrome (employees). The Veridyne Team browser extension shows an employee their own data from team.veridyne.eu (meetings, to-dos, hours and leave) and sends the hours, to-do ticks and leave requests they enter to that portal. It uses the employee's existing team portal sign-in, stores no credentials, sends nothing to third parties and does not read other websites.
  • Veridyne Admin app (iPhone and Android). The Veridyne Admin app is used only by the Veridyne Solutions owner account to run the business from a phone: approving hours and leave, answering access requests and looking up clients, projects and people. It signs in through auth.veridyne.eu with two-step verification. The app keeps the sign-in on the phone in the system keychain, readable only on that device. Face ID or the device passcode unlocks the app; the face data stays with the phone's operating system and never reaches the app or us. When notifications are turned on, we store the phone's notification token so we can tell the owner that something new is waiting. The token is deleted at sign-out, or when Apple or Google report that the app was removed. Documents opened in the app are kept only until the app locks. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own domain.

Legal basis

We process contact form data to take steps at your request before a possible contract, and for our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Server logs are processed for our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)). Sign-in and notification data for the Veridyne Admin app are processed for our legitimate interest in running our business securely (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel Inc. hosts this website.
  • Resend delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs Turnstile on auth.veridyne.eu, the sign-in page for Veridyne apps, to tell people from bots. It sees your IP address and browser details when you sign up or sign in there, and also uses them to improve its bot detection, as a separate controller. It is not used on this website.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email.
  • Okta, Inc. (Auth0) runs auth.veridyne.eu, where you sign in to Veridyne apps.
  • Neon, Inc. hosts the databases behind our portals and apps.
  • Apple Inc. and Google LLC (Firebase Cloud Messaging) deliver notifications to the Veridyne Admin app on iPhone and Android.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer relies on the safeguards they provide, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. If you become a client, we keep correspondence for as long as the law requires us to keep business records.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Email solutions@veridyne.eu. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).
