Who we are

This website is run by VERIDYNE SOLUTIONS S.R.L. (CUI 51635423, Reg. Com. J2025027184005), Str. Guliver nr. 13, Bucharest, Romania. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: solutions@veridyne.eu.

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, company (if you give it), the topic you pick and your message. We use them only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to prepare and manage our work together.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records technical data such as IP address, browser and time of request, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Veridyne Team for Chrome (employees). The Veridyne Team browser extension shows an employee their own data from team.veridyne.eu (meetings, to-dos, hours and leave) and sends the hours, to-do ticks and leave requests they enter to that portal. It uses the employee's existing team portal sign-in, stores no credentials, sends nothing to third parties and does not read other websites.
  • Veridyne Secure AI. Veridyne Secure AI runs inside the client portal at my.veridyne.eu, for signed-in people of client companies. A company admin writes a description of their AI agent and answers a few questions, and the app builds an identity design from them. Before the description is saved or read, email addresses and phone numbers in it are removed automatically. The description is then sent to our AI provider, which reads it only to fill in the answers. The design itself is built by Veridyne's own rules, not by the AI. We keep the description, the answers, the design, and the name and email address of the person who built it, with the company's other portal data. A company admin can remove a design from the portal. When a company books a design review, we also keep the name and email address of the person who asked, the topics and the times they proposed, and a copy of the design as a PDF with the company's documents. Please do not put personal data about other people in the description.
  • Veridyne Neural Network. Veridyne Neural Network runs inside the client portal at my.veridyne.eu, for signed-in people of client companies that Veridyne has switched it on for. A company admin connects the company’s Auth0 tenant with a machine-to-machine application; its client secret is stored encrypted. The app reads the tenant’s applications, APIs and grants, and receives the tenant’s log events for token requests, logins and Management API changes through a log stream the admin sets up. It never changes the tenant’s applications, users or login. User IDs in those events are replaced by a keyed hash and used only to count how many people an agent acted for; they are never shown and are deleted after 30 days. IP addresses are shortened before they are stored. Log events are kept for 30 days, daily counts for a year, and findings for a year after they are closed. We also keep the owner names and email addresses the admin enters for each agent, and the alert channels the admin sets up. Where a company has its own database with us, this data is kept there.
  • Veridyne Transformer. Veridyne Transformer runs at transformer.veridyne.eu for signed-in people of client companies and Veridyne staff. To plan a migration it reads the configuration of an identity tenant (applications, policies, groups and settings) through a read-only key the person creates, or from an export file they upload. It does not read user records: exports from Keycloak and PingFederate are reduced in the browser before anything is sent. Keys and secrets are stored encrypted, never shown back, and deleted when the person disconnects. We keep the project, the configuration read, the decisions made and the answers given, and the name and email address of the person who made them. When the person chooses to create objects on an Auth0 target, it uses a key they provide for that and only creates objects; it never changes or deletes existing ones.
  • Alecsander for Chrome. The Alecsander browser extension warns about fake sign-in pages, lists where you signed in and points out apps that ask for broad access. It works entirely on your computer: we receive nothing from it, and its pages and background script are blocked from making any network request. To do its job it reads, on your computer only: the sign-in pages you open (page title, text near the sign-in form, logos and the address the form sends to), the addresses and settings of sign-in requests (such as the app's client ID, the requested permissions and the identity provider), and the name, domain and end date of session cookies. It never reads or keeps what you type, passwords, tokens, sign-in codes or cookie values. What it keeps is stored in your browser: the places you signed in to, permissions apps asked for, its warnings, the sites you chose to trust and, only if you press "Look for existing sign-ins", a list of sites where your browser holds a sign-in. Records older than 30 days are deleted automatically; Settings lets you export or delete everything, and removing the extension deletes it all. "Clear session" and "Sign out here" delete that site's cookies in your browser, only when you press them. The extension has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and its data is not sold, shared or used for anything else.
  • Veridyne Admin app (iPhone and Android). The Veridyne Admin app is used only by the Veridyne Solutions owner account to run the business from a phone: approving hours and leave, answering access requests and looking up clients, projects and people. It signs in through auth.veridyne.eu with two-step verification. The app keeps the sign-in on the phone in the system keychain, readable only on that device. Face ID or the device passcode unlocks the app; the face data stays with the phone's operating system and never reaches the app or us. When notifications are turned on, we store the phone's notification token so we can tell the owner that something new is waiting. The token is deleted at sign-out, or when Apple or Google report that the app was removed. Documents opened in the app are kept only until the app locks. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own domain.

Legal basis

We process contact form data to take steps at your request before a possible contract, and for our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Server logs are processed for our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)). Sign-in and notification data for the Veridyne Admin app are processed for our legitimate interest in running our business securely (Art. 6(1)(f)). Descriptions, designs and review requests in Veridyne Secure AI are processed to provide the service the client company asked for and to prepare the design work it may order (Art. 6(1)(b)), and for our legitimate interest in running that service (Art. 6(1)(f)). Tenant data in Veridyne Neural Network is processed to provide the service the client company asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)); the client company is responsible for the people in its own tenant, and we act on its behalf. Tenant configuration in Veridyne Transformer is processed to provide the migration plan the person asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
