Veridyne Solutions is an identity company in Bucharest. Our work is Okta, Auth0 and the identity and access management around them: how your customers and staff sign in, and who can reach what.

How we do identity right.

  1. Secure by default.Passkeys and MFA where your users can take them, short-lived tokens, least privilege.
  2. Agreed before built.Scope, timeline and price in writing before any work starts.
  3. In the open.The work happens on your tenant and you see every change.
  4. Yours to keep.Full documentation at handover, and we stay reachable after.
